    About this demo

    Rainy Days is an educational student project. Please use fictional information when testing the checkout form.

    Shopping cart storage

    The website uses localStorage under the key rainyDaysCart to save product IDs and quantities in your browser. This allows your cart to remain available between pages and browser sessions. Completing a demo purchase clears the cart.

    Order confirmation storage

    The website uses sessionStorage under the key rainyDaysOrder to save a demo order reference, creation time, total quantity and total price. This information is used to display the confirmation page and is normally cleared when the browser tab is closed.

    Checkout form

    The demo asks for a name, email address and address to demonstrate a checkout form. The checkout JavaScript does not send these details to a server or include them in the stored order confirmation. No payment details are requested.

    External services

    Product data and images are requested from Noroff services. These requests share technical connection information, such as your IP address, with the services receiving them. The website hosting provider may also process technical request logs.

    Managing browser storage

    You can delete the cart and demo order information through your browser's site data settings. Removing or blocking browser storage may prevent the cart and checkout from working.
