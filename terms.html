About this Website

Rainy Days is a student projects created for educational purposes. It is a demonstration store and does not sell or deliver products.

Products and prices

Product information and images are supplied by the Noroff Rainy Days API. This demo displays the supplied price amount in NOK. Product information and prices may change.

Shopping cart

You can add jackets to your cart and remove them before completing a demo order. The cart is saved in your browser so that it remains available when you visit another page or refresh the website.

Demo checkout

Checkout simulates placing an order. No payment is collected, no products are reserved or shipped, and no confirmation email is sent. Please use fictional information in the checkout form.

Availability

The website depends on an external API. Products may temporarily be unavailable if the API or your internet connection is interrupted.
